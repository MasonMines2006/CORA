What period does a small positive insertion give you?

When the reactivity you add is small compared with β̄eff, the prompt neutrons barely matter. The period is set by how fast the delayed-neutron precursors decay, so you can use the short form:

Worked example. Add ρ = +0.001 Δk/k with β̄eff = 0.0065:

  1. 1τ ≈ (0.0065 − 0.001) / (0.1 × 0.001)
  2. 2τ ≈ 0.0055 / 0.0001 = 55 s
  3. 3SUR = 26.06 / τ ≈ 0.47 DPM

Power rises by a factor of e every 55 seconds, a little under half a decade per minute. That is the number the startup-rate meter shows you, and it is the arithmetic behind the SUR limits written into startup procedures.

Before you continue

If you doubled the insertion to ρ = +0.002, would the period roughly halve, or shrink by more than half?

More than half. τ ≈ 0.0045 / 0.0002 ≈ 23 s. The numerator shrinks too, so the period falls faster than 1/ρ.
