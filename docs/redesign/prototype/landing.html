What sets the −80 s period after a scram?

C

The longest-lived delayed-neutron precursor group. Its ~55 s half-life means power can't fall faster than about −80 s once the prompt drop is over RTM ch.4 · ¶167.

“…the rate of power decrease is limited by the decay of the longest-lived precursor group, resulting in a period of approximately −80 seconds.”
